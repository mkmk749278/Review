### 1. Critical Red Flags (Must Fix Prior to Render/Launch)
* **[Whole film]: 0 black flash frames (v8 had 39). No dark stretch ≥ 0.5 s.** Pass.
* **[13:35.13–13:35.17] and [21:37.98–21:38.00]: three single-frame shots in a row inside the Sanand plant clip.** The
  clip's own cut sits at 62.3 s in the source, and the shot starts at 60 s. On a phone this reads as a stutter. The
  same frames appear twice, because both shots start at 60 s.
  **Fix:** start each shot between the source's cuts (scene cuts at 50.92 / 59.73 / 75.77 / 79.85 s): 51.3 s for "she
  gets the job" (workers on the line), 75.85 s for "the work does not stop" (paint shop). This also ends the repeat.

  including a 2-frame cut at 3.20 s in the source. **Fix:** use only the sky-and-falling-car shot (source 1.45 s, at 0.56
  speed), ending before 3.20 s.
* **[16:10.52–16:11.05]: shots of 3 and 2 frames in the Nano launch clip** (source cuts at 114.40 and 114.92 s).
  **Fix:** start at 115.0 s, where the car drives out of the dark into the lights. That is one 5 s shot, and it shows
  the words "drives a tiny car into the television lights" exactly.
* **[16:37–16:41]: "TATA" laid across the WELCOME TO LAND ROVER sign reads "LCOME TO TATA D ROVER".** **Fix:** set the
  word over the plant, right of the sign.
