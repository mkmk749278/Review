### 1. Critical Red Flags (Must Fix Prior to Render/Launch)

* **[Whole film, 40 shots / 263 s]: Judder (24/25 fps footage in a 30 fps timeline).** Every 5th–6th frame is a duplicate, so motion stutters at a steady beat. On a phone this reads as "the frame skips". Worst stretches:
  0:11–0:21, 0:29–0:37, 0:58–1:06, 1:11–1:19, 1:47–2:06, 2:12–2:32, 2:42–3:01, 3:12–3:16, 4:02–4:08, 5:49–5:58, 6:10–6:30, 7:12–7:43, 7:55–8:13, 8:30–8:35, 8:47–9:00, 9:44–10:07, 10:08–10:10, 10:32–10:34, 10:42–11:32.
  It hits stock and Lyfius clips (shot at 25 fps) and the animated scenes rendered at 24/25 fps alike (the tank diagram, the maps, the capsule, the end card).
  **Fix:**
  - Conform every source to 30 fps before it enters the timeline. For real footage use motion-compensated interpolation:
    `ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -vf "minterpolate=fps=30:mi_mode=mci:mc_mode=aobmc:me_mode=bidir:vsbmc=1" -c:v libx264 -crf 16 out_30.mp4`
    Check the result for warping on fast motion; fall back to `framerate=fps=30` (blend) where it warps.
  - Re-render all animated scenes natively at 30 fps (or 60). Never resample them.
  - Better still for the 4K master: author and render at 60 fps, with the stock clips interpolated to 60.

  **Fix:** snap every cut to whole 30 fps frames after conforming. Re-check by measuring that no shot shorter than 10 frames remains unless it is meant to be there.

* **[2:05.5 and 2:12.5]: ~1 s of empty teal background between scenes** (a "void" frame with no picture). It reads as a missing shot. **Fix:** overlap the scenes, or extend the outgoing clip.

  - At 6:38: `1991 · ALL MEDICINE POWDER IMPORTS · CHINA <1 %`

Protocol Pillar 3: trade lines end at real entry ports. **Fix:**
  - For the general China lines, drop the HUBEI label (or mark a generic "CHINA").
  - End the line at JNPT/Nhava Sheva or Chennai port, with a small port label.
* **Mechanism (4:24–5:20): correct and well pitched.**
